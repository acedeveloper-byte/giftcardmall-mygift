How to check a GiftCardMall balance

Use the balance-check instructions provided with your own card. The back of the card, its packaging or the original issuer documentation is the best place to begin. Confirm the website address before entering sensitive information.

  1. Find the card’s instructions

    Review the reverse side, packaging and purchase documents for the designated service method.

  2. Verify the web address

    Check spelling and the complete domain. Do not rely only on an advertisement or an unexpected message.

  3. Allow for recent activity

    Purchases, pending authorizations, refunds and adjustments may affect the displayed available value.

Remember: A card’s remaining balance may differ from the original amount loaded onto it. Follow the terms and servicing directions for your specific card.

GiftCardMall/MyGift Visa and prepaid cards

A search containing “Visa” may relate to a prepaid card that uses the Visa payment network. Whether a particular purchase will work depends on the card’s terms, the merchant, the available value and the type of transaction.

Merchant acceptance

The merchant must accept the card network and the transaction type.

Available funds

Transaction limits

Some card programs restrict online, international or recurring payments.

Why might a prepaid gift card be declined?

A rejected payment does not necessarily mean the card has no remaining value. Possible causes include:

  • Insufficient available balance or a pending authorization.
  • The merchant does not accept that card type.
  • Incorrect payment details or an activation requirement.
  • Restrictions on the transaction category or location.
  • A purchase, refund or adjustment that has not fully processed.

What to do next

Review the current available balance, confirm the merchant’s accepted payment methods, check transaction details and consult the original card instructions. If the issue continues, contact the issuer through verified contact details supplied with the card.

Keep your card details private

Prepaid cards can hold monetary value, so treat their details as sensitive information.

  • Use the servicing instructions supplied with your card.
  • Verify the domain before entering private information.
  • Keep purchase receipts and card documentation.
  • Be cautious with unsolicited balance-check links.
  • Never post your full card number, PIN, CVV or account password on an informational website.
